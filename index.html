Tao Huang 黄涛

"The only way to do great work is to love what you do."

I am a tenure-track assistant professor in the John Hopcroft Center for Computer Science, School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University since 2025. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from The University of Sydney, advised by Prof. Chang Xu. Before that, I obtained my Bachelor's degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, and spent two years as a Researcher at SenseTime. I was selected as a Microsoft Research Asia StarTrack Scholar in 2026, and received the Shanghai Magnolia Talent Plan Youth Project in 2025 and the NeurIPS Scholar Award in 2023.

Our lab focuses on real-time interactive intelligence, spanning two interaction frontiers — interactive audiovisual worlds (audio/video generation, interactive world models, and code-driven media) and embodied physical AI (world-action models, agentic embodied AI, and humanoid control) — grounded in two core foundations: efficient AI for real-time responsiveness and recursive self-improvement (RSI) for continual capability evolution (see Research).
