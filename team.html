There are currently no PhD or Master openings for the 2027 intake. I am actively recruiting motivated research interns in the following areas:

  • Audio & Video Generation: general audio/video synthesis, interactive audiovisual world models, and code-driven media generation
  • Embodied AI: world-action models (WAM), agentic embodied AI, VLAs, and humanoid control
  • Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): self-improving LLM / MLLM agents and embodied agents
  • Large Model Acceleration: efficient architectures (linear attention, SSMs), token compression/caching, and knowledge distillation

Interested candidates are welcome to reach out at t.huang@sjtu.edu.cn with the subject line Intern / RA Application – [Name]. I try to clear my inbox once per day.
